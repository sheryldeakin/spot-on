| 2026-10-02 | Two model consultations on the control-character guard, run headless with the same written brief: OpenAI Codex (gpt-5.6-sol) and Google Gemini (Antigravity CLI) | no URL; briefs and replies kept in the session scratchpad | secondary | Review of `tests/test_docs.py` control-character check, `~/.claude/scripts/check_control_chars.py` and the rule behind them.
